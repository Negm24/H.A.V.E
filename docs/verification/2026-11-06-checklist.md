# AutoPharm requirement-to-evidence checklist — 6 November 2026

Read with the [readiness report and evidence ledger](2026-11-06-readiness.md).
Source: [current requirements](../../01-requirements.md), including latest superseding decisions and the requested automatic QR change.

All 216 explicitly numbered requirements are listed: 152 FR, 25 NFR, 18 SEC, 9 REG and 12 PRV. Gaps in source numbering are intentional; no requirements were invented to fill them.

**Verified** means the described implemented behavior has current evidence, with the stated limits. It does not mean production certification, external provider acceptance, physical hardware acceptance, or every future clause of the broader domain. **Simulated** identifies a deliberate implemented simulation; a missing feature intended for simulation is **Planned**. Section 5 DOCUMENT requirements are listed without turning legal assertions into verified facts.

Historical basket statements have explicit superseding decisions and are not unresolved product choices. Eventual printer/saved-card-removal scope is discussed in the report. No unresolved specification conflict was used to override current explicit user requirements.
